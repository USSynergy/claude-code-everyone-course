# Leadership Update

## Basecamp Rewards — Program Assessment
**Date:** April 2024
**From:** New Manager

### Status
At Risk

### Summary
After reviewing 4 months of customer feedback and inherited program data, the core problem is clear: customers don't dislike Basecamp Rewards — they simply forget it exists. The program has no personality, no engagement loop, and no connection to the Basecamp brand that customers already love. The gap between overall brand NPS (67) and program NPS (12) tells the story.

| Program NPS | 12 | 40+ | Down |
| Brand NPS | 67 | — | Stable |
